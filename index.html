Theorem: Sum of angles of a quadrilateral is 360°

Formal Proof

Given: A quadrilateral ABCD.

To Prove: ∠A + ∠B + ∠C + ∠D = 360°

Construction: Join diagonal AC to divide quadrilateral ABCD into two triangles: ▵ADC and ▵ABC.

1. In ▵ADC: ∠DAC + ∠ACD + ∠D = 180°  (Angle sum of ▵) ...(i)
2. In ▵ABC: ∠CAB + ∠ACB + ∠B = 180°  (Angle sum of ▵) ...(ii)
3. Adding (i) & (ii):
(∠DAC + ∠CAB) + (∠ACD + ∠ACB) + ∠B + ∠D = 180° + 180°
⇒ ∠A + ∠C + ∠B + ∠D = 360°  (Hence Proved)
